# Community conduct

Contributors and visitors are welcome to challenge claims, reproduce results and
propose corrections. Discuss the work and its evidence respectfully. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, impersonation, doxxing and publication of private
information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close unproductive discussions,
or restrict participation. Responses should be proportionate and focus on keeping
substantive scientific and philosophical discussion possible. Good-faith criticism
and disagreement are not misconduct.

Use GitHub's reporting tools for abusive content. Do not repeat private or harmful
material in a public issue merely to report it.
