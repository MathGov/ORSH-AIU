## Frame-Aligned Records and Absolute Infinite Union

Open research on quantum records, locality, and philosophical foundations.
By **James McGaughran**, MathGov / RippleLogic Research Program.

**[Read online](https://mathgov.github.io/ORSH-AIU/) ·
[Download the collection](https://github.com/MathGov/ORSH-AIU/releases/latest) ·
[Reproduce results](REPRODUCING.md) · [Cite this work](CITATION.md)**

This is the standalone home for the finalized v1.8 research collection.
The scientific work develops safeguards for comparing locality and records
in quantum models. The AIU manuscript develops a separate philosophical
argument. Neither numerical reproduction nor public release establishes
the philosophical thesis, a spacetime metric, or a consciousness result.

### What the results say

The original six-qubit source-off pilot's predeclared readability-change mean
is **−0.015225**. Later paired factorial comparisons ask a different question:
they compare Hamiltonian orientations at fixed injection and yield positive
contrasts near **0.0105 and 0.0126** at six qubits. These are compatible signs
for different estimands. Hamiltonian rotation is not a locality-only
intervention. See [the evidence guide](EVIDENCE.md) before reusing a headline result.
