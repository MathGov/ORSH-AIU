# Evidence and interpretation

## Scientific work

Frame-Aligned Records develops access-aware equivalence, valid inferential
targets and uncertainty-aware record certification. Tensor-product structures,
graphs, model laws and operational access assumptions are supplied. The work
does not reconstruct spacetime or introduce a verified decoherence mechanism.

The original pilot contains 192 independent model units (96 at four qubits,
96 at six), with paired evaluations and 101 checkpoints. At six qubits the
rotated readability-change mean is −0.015225, with 68 negative and 28 positive
units. The distribution-free 95% Hoeffding interval crosses zero. Narrower
bootstrap intervals are approximate. This is numerical simulation, not a
quantum-hardware experiment.

The later factorial extension compares two Hamiltonian orientations while
holding injection fixed. Its positive six-qubit contrasts near 0.0105 and
0.0126 do not reverse the earlier result: the counterfactuals differ.
Finite-family uncertainty statements condition on the declared sampling law
and numerical evaluation. Rotation changes more than locality alone.

## Philosophical work

Absolute Infinite Union distinguishes totality, monist grounding,
unboundedness, generation, plenitude, experiential fundamentality and cosmic
subjectivity. Its conditional probability and coverage arguments do not
establish a physical universe, consciousness or a numerical probability for AIU.
Empirical underdetermination and alternative accounts remain explicit.

## What verification establishes

Hashes establish byte identity. Structural checks compare delivered source
and DOCX content/math counts. Selected numerical checks compare methods on
finite inputs. Replays test agreement under stated versions and tolerances.
None is external peer review or a proof of the philosophical thesis.

Original verification files in `publication/verification/` describe the source
package's checks. New CI logs and `provenance/publication-checks.json` describe
the checks run for this open-publication edition. Do not conflate the two.
