# Deferred research catalogue

Preserved from W05 v1.1, with no new projects activated. The active task budget is in W05 v1.2.

## 5. Thirty additional directions, catalogued but not activated

These are questions for later work, not claims of novelty or promises that each route is tractable. Each has a trigger that would justify returning to it. They are deliberately short so that the programme does not spend its effort expanding every adjacent topic.

- **D01. Operator-norm locality:** Would a worst-case norm change candidate rankings? Reactivate when Hilbert-Schmidt residuals demonstrably miss a target effect.
- **D02. Low-energy locality:** How should the score be restricted to an effective energy sector? Reactivate with an actual low-energy recovery model.
- **D03. Approximate covariance:** What stationarity bound follows from bounded covariance error? Reactivate when numerical or experimental preparations violate exact symmetry.
- **D04. Open-system preparation:** Which covariance and record results survive for channels? Reactivate with a specified dissipative model and fixed-point structure.
- **D05. Relational clocks:** Can conditional records evolve in a stationary global state? Reactivate with a concrete clock-system model and operational observables.
- **D06. Gauge-constrained subsystems:** How should factorization be replaced by physical subalgebras? Reactivate with a gauge model whose constraints obstruct naive tensor factors.
- **D07. Fermionic locality:** Which access and parity constraints change the score? Reactivate with a fermionic benchmark rather than borrowing qubit assumptions.
- **D08. Source-leakage bounds:** How much record advantage comes from direct access to the encoding? Reactivate when candidate mixing makes source exclusion ambiguous.
- **D09. Continuous-time persistence:** Can sparse checkpoints bound error throughout a window? Reactivate when an analytic regularity bound is available.
- **D10. Record disturbance:** How does reading one fragment alter other records? Reactivate with an explicit measurement-instrument family.
- **D11. Redundancy versus independence:** Which correlations invalidate an objectivity claim? Reactivate with a stronger target than multiple decodable fragments.
- **D12. Search-budget correction:** How should many candidate graphs and spans be penalized? Reactivate after a fixed candidate generator and fitting procedure exist.
- **D13. Candidate metric geometry:** Can a meaningful distance between subsystem structures be computed? Reactivate when equivalence classes and access costs are fixed.
- **D14. Finite-size scaling:** Does an apparent compatibility persist as system size grows? Reactivate after a nontrivial small-system result and cost estimate.
- **D15. Alternative null groups:** Which restricted permutations preserve physical eligibility? Reactivate when uniform exchangeability can be shown to fail.
- **D16. A physical link-breaking intervention:** Can alignment change while relevant resources are matched? Reactivate with a causal model and measurable balance tolerances.
- **D17. Quantum causal-order growth:** Do record observables coexist with geometric recovery in a specified growth model? Reactivate with a quantum dynamics, not only classical order statistics.
- **D18. Holographic record reconstruction:** Which boundary records correspond to bulk locality? Reactivate in a concrete duality with an explicit recovery map.
- **D19. Thermodynamic cost of records:** What resources are required for formation, stabilization and erasure? Reactivate with a defined work/heat accounting convention.
- **D20. Infinite-domain limit:** Does a family of finite models converge to a useful infinite one? Reactivate when topology, observables and convergence are specified.
- **D21. Finite versus infinite observational equivalence:** Which larger-domain measurement separates a chosen model pair? Reactivate with different likelihoods, not abstract size labels.
- **D22. Correlated plenitude:** Which dependence conditions still yield repeated finite patterns? Reactivate with a named mixing or ergodic process.
- **D23. Algorithmic generativity:** Does computational universality imply any physical realization claim? Reactivate with resource limits and an implementation map.
- **D24. Observer-selection measures:** Which measure predicts orderly records without pathological typicality? Reactivate with an actual ensemble and observer definition.
- **D25. Subject individuation in a common ground:** What makes two perspectives numerically distinct? Reactivate with a proposed bridge rule that differs from a rival.
- **D26. Split and merged access:** Which experiential predictions follow when information access divides or combines? Reactivate with a constrained subject theory and ethically available observations.
- **D27. Cross-cultural unity measurement:** Which dimensions remain comparable after translation? Reactivate with adequate groups, codebooks and an approved analysis design.
- **D28. Unity and lasting change:** Are transient episodes distinct from enduring behavioural or experiential changes? Reactivate with prospective longitudinal measures.
- **D29. Valence limits and recurrence:** What assumptions connect repeated patterns to welfare or personal continuation? Reactivate with explicit experience, valence and identity theories.
- **D30. Decision governance under ontological uncertainty:** Which protections remain invariant across plausible ontologies? Reactivate with fixed normative premises and concrete decisions, not cosmological scores.
