# Contributing

Corrections, criticism and reproduction reports are welcome through issues and
pull requests. Include the paper/version, section or equation, exact claim,
supporting argument and proposed correction. Numerical reports should include
operating system, Python/package versions, command, seed/configuration, observed
output and expected output. Share minimal reproducible examples without secrets.

Keep scientific and philosophical claims distinct. Do not describe a successful
same-code replay as independent replication. Historical files in `publication/`
are frozen: corrections belong in a new documented edition with fresh hashes,
not silent replacements. Website and guide corrections can be ordinary PRs.

Contributions to research documentation/data are under CC BY 4.0; contributions
to original software are under Apache 2.0. Submit only material you have the
right to contribute and preserve attribution. Respectful substantive disagreement
is welcome; harassment, impersonation and personal attacks are not.
