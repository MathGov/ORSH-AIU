# Changelog

## 1.8-open.1 — 27 September 2026

First standalone open-publication edition of the finalized v1.8 collection.
Adds CC BY 4.0 / Apache 2.0 licensing, five browser readers, search, a download
catalogue, citations, maintained reproduction guidance and automated checks.
Research manuscripts, numerical inputs, delivered outputs and original PDFs/DOCX
remain byte-for-byte unchanged. The source ZIP retains its original checksum.
Historical bundles and review correspondence are archive assets rather than
duplicated in the normal checkout. See `provenance/source-inventory.json`.

Earlier research changes remain in `publication/CHANGELOG.md` and the complete
archive. This public edition changes distribution and licensing, not findings.
